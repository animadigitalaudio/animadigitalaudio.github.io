How buying works

  1. Try it

    Download the full plug-in. Only the time sound runs through it counts toward the free hour.

  2. Buy through Creem

    Our reseller handles payment and VAT. Your license key appears right away and arrives by e-mail.

  3. Activate

    Paste the key into the plug-in, or use the activation page for a computer without internet.

  4. Make music

    From then on the plug-in works offline. Your trial projects open exactly as you left them.

No account, no iLok
